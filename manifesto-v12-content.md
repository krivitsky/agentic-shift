## NEW — insert here, before the shifts

### What we optimize for

Everyone is optimizing for something.

The frontier labs optimize for adoption and throughput. That is legitimate, and it is their business — not yours.

We optimize for two things: the value delivered to customers, and the health of the organization that delivers it — its capacity to keep learning, keep judging, and keep changing direction. Where those two diverge from adoption and throughput, we follow ours.

### What we refuse

We do not treat the loss of judgment as progress.

We do not accept dependence as a design outcome.

We do not confuse volume of output with capability.
